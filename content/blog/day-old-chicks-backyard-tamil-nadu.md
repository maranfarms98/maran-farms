A backyard flock in Tamil Nadu is usually 10–25 birds, a courtyard or terrace coop, and a WhatsApp thread — not a 2,000-bird shed. [Day-old chicks](/product/day-old-chick) from Maran Farms are hatched on our Karur farm and boxed the same morning. That timing is the product. A chick that sits two days in a warehouse is not a chick we will put our name on.

## What “day-old” actually means

We dispatch the morning after hatch, after a viability check. If the batch looks weak, we delay. Coimbatore, Erode, and Salem are the usual poultry roads; Chennai is a longer, hotter trip and we only send when someone will be home to receive the box.

[Country chicks](/product/country-chick) are the hardy nattu-kozhi type for keepers who want a bird that forages and takes heat. Standard day-old batches suit mixed backyard starts. Tell us which you want in the first message — we will not swap breeds in transit.

## First week

Brood under a reliable heat source. Keep water clean. Expect some first-week loss even in a good batch; we say that before you pay. Dogs, kites, and open drains kill more backyard chicks than genetics. A secure run for the first weeks is not optional.

Feed a starter crumble, then let country birds scratch. Do not dump 40 chicks into a yard on day two because a neighbour did it in 1998.

## How long until eggs or meat

This is not a two-week broiler cycle unless you asked for a broiler line. Layers take **roughly 5–6 months** to mature depending on breed mix and feed. Country chicks are slower than commercial hybrids. Plan months, not a festival date three weeks out.

## Combining with a fodder order

We will combine a chick crate with [Napier sticks](/category/napier-plants) only when both are ready the same morning. We will not hold chicks to wait for a later harvest. If you farm in [Pollachi](/delivery/pollachi) or [Salem](/delivery/salem), say the taluk so the departure hour matches the road.

Start small. Message the count, the district, and whether this is a first flock. We would rather send 12 healthy birds than 40 from a tired batch.
