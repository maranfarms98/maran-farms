Tamil Nadu dairy keepers ask this every planting season: **CO-4 or Red Napier?** Both are hybrid Napier sticks. Both cut in about 45–60 days with moisture. They are not the same crop in the pail.

## What CO-4 is for

CO-4 is the name farmers already know from TNAU talk and neighbour plots. It is the all-rounder: decent biomass, decent protein, a planting calendar that works year-round under drip, and a slightly lower stick price than Red Napier. If you are filling a first half-acre and you want a variety the farmhand has planted before, start with [CO-4 Napier](/product/co4-napier).

Rainfed plots still prefer a June–September plant. Irrigated Pollachi and Coimbatore dairies plant whenever the field is free.

## What Red Napier is for

[Red Napier](/product/red-napier) is the dairy leaf. Tillering is denser, protein in the chop is higher, and it is the stick Pollachi milk producers reorder when yield on ordinary grass has flattened. You pay a little more per stick. You cut at knee-to-waist height the same as CO-4 — do not let either stand go to full flowering if you want milk, not fibre.

A working ration is still about 40–50 kg of green fodder per milch animal per day once the plot is in regular harvest. Variety does not replace area. It changes how much protein is in that area.

## How we tell buyers to choose

- **Trial both.** 500 sticks of CO-4 and 500 of Red Napier on the same soil is cheaper than guessing wrong for a full acre.
- **Need volume on a budget?** Look at [Super Napier](/product/super-napier) for the bulk block and keep Red Napier on a dairy strip.
- **Already running CO-4 and want a bump?** Trial [CO-5](/product/co5-napier) or Red Napier on a few rows, not the whole field.

## Planting, not YouTube

Sticks at 45°, 2–3 feet between rows, plant within 48 hours of delivery, manure at planting, top-dress after the first cut. That list does not change between CO-4 and Red Napier. What changes is the leaf you get if you actually harvest on time.
